## EPIC Summer School

**EPIC (Exploring particle Physics Integrated with Computing) Summer School** &mdash;
July 13&ndash;17, 2026, University of Mississippi

A week-long training programme introducing high school and undergraduate participants
to particle physics and scientific computing, supported by the Mississippi EPSCoR
Scientific Hub (MESH). Dr. Davies coordinated recruitment, curriculum development, and
student mentorship.

- School website: [olemiss-physics.github.io/epic-summer-2026](https://olemiss-physics.github.io/epic-summer-2026/)
- Site repository: [github.com/olemiss-physics/epic-summer-2026](https://github.com/olemiss-physics/epic-summer-2026)
- Curriculum notebooks: [github.com/gavinsdavies/MESH](https://github.com/gavinsdavies/MESH)
