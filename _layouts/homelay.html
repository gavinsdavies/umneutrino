Established in 2019

Neutrino physics from Oxford, Mississippi to Fermilab and beyond

Our group works on neutrino oscillation physics, detector data analysis, and hadron-production measurements that support long-baseline experiments. We are grateful for support from the University of Mississippi and the U.S. Department of Energy.

Join, visit, or collaborate

We welcome conversations with prospective graduate students, postdoctoral researchers, undergraduate researchers, and collaborators interested in neutrino physics, software, and analysis.

Research focus

Experiments we help make possible

NOvA

Precision long-baseline oscillation measurements using Fermilab's NuMI beam and detectors in Illinois and Minnesota.

DUNE

Next-generation measurements of mass ordering, CP violation, supernova neutrinos, and rare processes.

EMPHATIC

Hadron-production data that reduce neutrino-beam uncertainties for current and future oscillation experiments.
